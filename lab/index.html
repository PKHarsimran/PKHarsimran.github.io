    A COUPLE OF FIELD NOTES

    Does this scan my device?

    No. Pixel reacts to the page, and the route is an illustration. The cases use training data. The optional IP button asks ipify for your internet-facing address; it is not a device scan or a way to identify you.

    What gets saved?

    Your random visitor pass, solved cases, tour progress, and motion preference stay in this browser. The lab does not send your answers to a server. The IP lookup only runs when you ask, is not saved by the lab, and is never added to the badge. Read the site privacy policy.
